Add `@nebutra/fonts/next/display`, a home for display italics that the upright-only registry cannot hold, starting with Fraunces Italic (Latin subset, SIL OFL 1.1, `preload: false`). `@nebutra/tokens/fonts/registry` now also exports `fontDisplayClassName`. The design site sets "Design" in its header in that face.
