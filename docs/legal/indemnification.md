# Indemnification (Enterprise)

> ⚠️ **This is a term sheet, not an executed contract.** It describes the
> indemnity we are prepared to offer under an Enterprise agreement, so that
> your legal team can assess it before we spend time on paper. The binding
> text is the executed master agreement or order form. **Both parties should
> have counsel review before signing.**

## Why this exists

Neither the FSL-1.1-ALv2 nor the MIT licence carries any indemnity — both
disclaim all warranties and liability. That is normal for freely licensed
software, and it is also the single most common reason an enterprise legal
review blocks adoption: nobody is on the hook if a third party claims the code
infringes their rights.

An Enterprise agreement supplies that missing counterparty.

## What we indemnify

Under an Enterprise agreement, Wuxi Nebutra Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd.
("Nebutra") will defend the customer against, and pay damages finally awarded
in, any third-party claim alleging that **the Software as delivered by us**
infringes that third party's copyright, trademark, trade secret, or patent
rights.

This covers:

- The source code we authored and distribute in this repository
- The packages we publish to npm under `@nebutra/*`, `nebutra`, `create-sailor`
- Our selection and integration of third-party open-source dependencies

## What we do not indemnify

Precision here protects both sides. The indemnity does **not** cover claims
arising from:

1. **Your modifications** — code you or a third party changed, added, or
   removed, where the claim would not have arisen without the change.
2. **Your combinations** — the claim arises from combining the Software with
   other software, data, or hardware we did not supply.
3. **Third-party services you select** — LLM providers, payment processors,
   auth providers, databases. You contract with those vendors directly and
   their terms govern.
4. **Content and data you process** — anything your users or your business put
   through the system, including AI-generated output.
5. **Continued use after notice** — using an allegedly infringing version after
   we have supplied a non-infringing update or workaround at no cost.
6. **Versions outside your support term** — including versions you obtained
   under the free Community terms rather than under the agreement.
7. **Trademark claims arising from your own branding**, including under a
   white-label licence, where the mark at issue is yours rather than ours.

## Process

1. **Notice** — you notify us promptly and in writing of the claim. Delay that
   materially prejudices the defence reduces our obligation accordingly.
2. **Control** — we have sole control of the defence and settlement, provided
   no settlement imposes a non-indemnified obligation or an admission of
   liability on you without your written consent.
3. **Cooperation** — you provide reasonable assistance at our expense.
4. **Mitigation** — if the Software becomes, or in our reasonable opinion is
   likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, we may at our
   option and expense: (a) procure the right for you to continue using it,
   (b) modify or replace it so it is non-infringing while materially
   preserving functionality, or (c) if neither is commercially reasonable,
   terminate the affected licence and refund the unused portion of prepaid
   fees.

## Liability cap

The indemnity is subject to the aggregate liability cap in the master
agreement, negotiated per engagement. Typical structure:

- **Standard cap** — 12 months of fees paid under the agreement
- **Elevated cap** — available at a premium where your risk model requires it
- **Uncapped** — mutual fraud, wilful misconduct, and death or personal injury,
  as required by applicable law

The IP indemnity may sit inside or outside the general cap; this is a
commercial negotiation and we are open to it sitting outside, priced
accordingly.

## Warranty

Alongside the indemnity, Enterprise agreements include a limited warranty that
the Software will perform materially in accordance with its documentation
during the term, with repair-replace-refund as the exclusive remedy.

Community and Team tiers carry **no warranty** — the licence disclaimer
applies in full.

## Insurance

Evidence of professional indemnity and cyber liability cover can be provided
on request during procurement. Coverage limits are stated in the certificate,
not here, so this page does not go stale.

## Not available at Community or Team tier

The Community tier is governed solely by [LICENSE](../../LICENSE), which
disclaims all warranties and liability. The Team tier adds support
commitments but no indemnity. Indemnification requires an executed Enterprise
agreement.
