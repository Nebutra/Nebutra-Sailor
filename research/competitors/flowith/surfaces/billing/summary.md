# flowith.billing — upgrade modal / credits

Single credit balance ('cr.') + per-model 'Free Gens' quotas + an Unlimited Pack for top models on Ultimate/Infinite. Starter = 300 cr./mo ≈ 4 Nano Banana Pro images. Client model catalog prices every model in internal credits (see raw/global-models.txt; text per token, image/video flat). 0-credit accounts get a persistent toast, an auto upgrade modal, in-node 'Need a refill?' cards, locked Edit/batch/models. Evidence: `evidence/billing.upgrade-modal.webp`.
